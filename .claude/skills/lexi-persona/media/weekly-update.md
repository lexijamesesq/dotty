# Medium: Weekly Update / Status

Manager-voice reporting to leadership and peers. The job is to show what changed, honestly.

## Structure

Each item: context (the before state) → what changed → evidence it worked. An update is a set of these connected by a through-line — not a list of disconnected activities.

## Thinking model this medium leans on

`activity-vs-impact` — the core discipline of the medium. Every item names a state change, not just effort.

## Attribution as evidence

Ground claims in who saw what: name the people whose reactions or quotes validate the change ("[name] noted…", "[name] shared…"). Attribution is evidence, not name-dropping — it shows the change registered with someone real. Names come from bound project context; in thin context, mark `[NEEDS: name]`.

## Calibrate

Report progress at its true size: "trending positively," "began to see the merits" — not "fixed," not "bought in." Honest self-assessment includes naming what didn't work. Do not hide challenges; do not inflate.

## Anti-patterns

- Activity lists masquerading as accomplishments ("I facilitated / ran / ensured").
- Disconnected items with no through-line.
- Taking sole credit for collaborative work.
- Cramming multiple unrelated things into one item.
