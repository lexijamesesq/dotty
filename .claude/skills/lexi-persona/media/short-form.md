# Medium: Slack Message / Email

Short-form, often ad hoc. Voice carries it — there is no section structure. Most short-form work is refine: tightening something already drafted.

## Moves

- Lead with what's actually different — no setup or framing sentence.
- Em-dash pivot straight to the implication: "not just X — Y."
- Name specifics — mechanisms, numbers, behaviors. State implications directly: "Their proposal: X." not "The practical upshot is that…"
- Short declarative sentences. Fragments are fine here when they land harder.

## Don't

- Wrap-up phrases: "the practical upshot is," "the key takeaway," "what this means for."
- Academic or formal phrasing.
- Polishing until it reads like a press release.
- Hedging or over-explaining.

## Email vs. Slack

Same voice. Email tolerates one more sentence of context up front when the recipient lacks it; Slack assumes shared context. Neither gets a preamble.
