# Priority vs. Solution

A priority is an outcome to pursue. A solution is one way to get there. They are not interchangeable, and stating a solution where a priority belongs quietly forecloses the thinking.

- **Solution masquerading as a priority:** "Build modular widgets." — that's an implementation; it assumes the answer.
- **Priority:** "Let people shape the surface to their own decisions." — an outcome; modular widgets are one way to reach it.

## Apply it

- When something is framed as a priority, test it: is this an outcome, or already a chosen build? If it names a mechanism, lift it to the outcome it serves.
- Keep solutions where they belong — in the "how," after the priority is set.
- A priorities section full of solutions has skipped the strategy step.

Medium-neutral: applies wherever priorities, plans, or roadmaps are stated.
