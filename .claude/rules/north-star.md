# North Star

The North Star is the outcome the work exists to produce, in the operator's words. It is checked at three boundaries — before engaging, before delegating, and before delivering.

## Before engaging

Articulate the North Star you will measure against — as an observable outcome, not an activity. If you cannot state it, work with the operator to define and clarify it. This includes any form of engagement: discussing an objective, planning work, executing, or validating.

## Before delegating

Act as operator to the subagent. State the North Star in the brief so the delegate can measure against it. The exception is blind-test scenarios where the delegate should not have it.

## Before delivering

Measure the work against the North Star before giving it to the operator. Hold the artifact against her words — not your restatement of them. If the work optimized for an adjacent target, say so and name the difference. Do not deliver work you know is off-target with a caveat attached.
